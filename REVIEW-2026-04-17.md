# Blacklist Domain Classification Review

**Date:** 2026-04-17
**Scope:** 200 randomly sampled domains from each of `bank/domains`, `bitcoin/domains`, and `gambling/domains` (600 total)
**Method:** Each domain was visited via HTTPS and its content assessed against the list's category. Unreachable sites were evaluated by domain name, web search, and historical context.

## Evaluation Criteria

The purpose of these lists is to control AI agent access to high-impact actions:

- **bank**: Blocks access to financial institutions and services. Banks, credit unions, payment processors, financial services platforms, and loan sites all count as correctly classified. Parked or hijacked domains are acceptable to block.
- **bitcoin**: Blocks access to cryptocurrency and blockchain services. Exchanges, mining pools, wallets, faucets, DeFi platforms, blockchain infrastructure, and crypto news sites all count as correctly classified.
- **gambling**: Blocks access to gambling and betting services. Casinos, sportsbooks, lotteries, poker, bingo, gambling affiliates, and review sites all count as correctly classified.

## Bank — 200 Domains Reviewed

### Result: 99.5% accuracy

The bank list is the cleanest of the three. Nearly every entry is a legitimate financial institution (active or defunct), credit union, payment processor, or financial services company.

### Removed

| Domain | Reason |
|--------|--------|
| `ica.se` | Swedish grocery retailer (ICA supermarkets). The banking subsidiary operates at `icabanken.se`. Blocking `ica.se` prevents grocery shopping, not banking. |

### Debatable — Banking Trade Associations

These three domains are banking industry advocacy organizations, not financial service providers. They don't process transactions or hold deposits. Whether to block them depends on the desired scope.

| Domain | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| `cbak.com` | Community Bankers Association of Kansas |
| `ibcbanks.org` | Independent Community Bankers of Colorado |
| `aseanbankers.org` | ASEAN Bankers Association |

### Notable Observations

- **Parked domains** (5 found): `canonbank.com`, `hondonationalbank.com`, `mainebank.com`, `southvalleybank.com`, `harborbankgroup.com`. All were historically bank domains now listed for sale. Acceptable to keep.
- **Hijacked domain** (1 found): `fricktricountyfcu.org` now serves a Vavada online casino. The legitimate FCU rebranded to `frickfin.org`. Acceptable to keep blocked.
- **`.bank` TLD migration**: Many US banks have moved to the `.bank` TLD (e.g., `pilgrimbank.bank`, `visionbank.bank`, `nve.bank`). The old `.com` domains still redirect correctly. Consider adding corresponding `.bank` domains.
- **`bankrate.com`** was flagged by review as a financial comparison/media site rather than a bank, but it fits the broader "financial services" scope since it facilitates loan and credit card applications.

## Gambling — 200 Domains Reviewed

### Result: 98% accuracy

The gambling list is large (32,242 domains) and dominated by disposable casino mirror domains on cheap TLDs (`.top`, `.buzz`, `.win`, `.xyz`). Approximately 60–70% of sampled domains are now dead (DNS NXDOMAIN), but their names are unambiguously gambling-related.

### Genuine False Positives

| Domain | What It Actually Is |
|--------|-------------------|
| `mkgamble.com` | **Law firm** — Law Office MK Gamble, LLC. "Gamble" is the attorney's surname. Estate planning, real estate, business law. |
| `casinopawnbrokers.com.au` | **Pawnshop** in Brisbane, Australia. "Casino" is part of the business name. Buys/sells gold and jewellery. |
| `supercasino.axome.com` | **French web agency** (Axome, Shopify Platinum Partner). Subdomain redirects to agency homepage. |
| `pub-79b7fbba87c04f4489bc2e0dd3bfd753.r2.dev` | **Generic Cloudflare R2 storage bucket**. Returns 404. No gambling content; could host anything. |

### Debatable

| Domain | Issue |
|--------|-------|
| `mgmresortsimages.com` | MGM Resorts image CDN. Not a gambling site itself, but owned by a casino company. |
| `mgmmiragevoice.com` | MGM corporate communications domain. Redirects to corporate portal. |
| `okcasinocareers.com` | Casino employment/careers site. Gambling-adjacent but not a gambling service. |
| `mrgmail.com` | Dead domain. Name looks like a gmail.com typosquat, not gambling-related. |
| `dayofshow.com` | Unreachable. Name suggests events/entertainment, not gambling. |

### Notable Observations

- **Extreme domain churn**: The gambling list contains thousands of ephemeral mirror domains (e.g., `solcasino-north.top`, `roxcasino-ms11.club`, `joycasino-zph5.top`). Most are already dead. This is the nature of grey-market online casino operations.
- **Keyword false positives**: `mkgamble.com` and `casinopawnbrokers.com.au` demonstrate the risk of pattern-matching on "gamble" and "casino" without semantic context. The word "casino" appears in legitimate business names unrelated to gambling.
- **`cartoonfile.net`** is a notable true positive — the innocuous domain name hides an Indonesian gambling slot site (JACKPOT168). This validates the list's value beyond simple keyword matching.
- **`illinoislottery.com`** (official state lottery) is correctly categorized as gambling.

## Recommendations

1. **Remove `ica.se` from `bank/domains`** — Done (committed).
2. **Consider removing genuine false positives** from `gambling/domains`: `mkgamble.com`, `casinopawnbrokers.com.au`, `supercasino.axome.com`, and the R2 bucket URL.
3. **Consider removing unrelated entries** from `bitcoin/domains`: `geetle.com`, `gus.host`, `dyqqq.com`, `tutor-web.info`.
4. **Monitor `.bank` TLD adoption** — many US banks are migrating and the list may need corresponding `.bank` entries.
5. **Periodic dead domain cleanup** is low priority — dead domains are harmless to keep and may be re-registered for their original purpose.
